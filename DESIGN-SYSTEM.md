# Design System Contract

## Brand

**APPROVED:** SwimShop Zimbabwe

## Colour

**APPROVED:** White, black and navy blue as the core brand palette.

## Visual direction

**APPROVED:** Premium athletic retail, clean, minimal, image-led, large whitespace and restrained colour.

## Typography

**PROVISIONAL:** Inter or another approved freely licensed high-quality sans serif. Final font selection belongs to the design-system milestone.

## Layout

**PROVISIONAL:** Maximum content width around 1440px, mobile gutters around 16px, larger desktop gutters, and a 4:5 product-card media ratio.

## Shape and motion

**PROVISIONAL:** Restrained corners and short 150–250ms UI motion. Reduced-motion preferences must disable or minimize nonessential motion.

## Asset rules

Use SwimShop Zimbabwe-owned or properly licensed imagery. Neutral placeholders are acceptable during foundation work. Do not use Arena proprietary assets.
